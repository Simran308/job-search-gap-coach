# Job Search Gap Coach

**Apply smarter, not more.** This is a weekly routine you can run in any AI assistant, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. It shows where you fit, which gaps are costing you replies, and how to close them quickly without inventing anything.

Works in any field. There are no paid automation tools to set up.

---

## What you need
- Your resume (paste it in or upload it)
- The job descriptions you applied to (pasted text or links)
- The replies you got: rejections, recruiter emails, assessment invites. You can paste them, or connect your inbox if your assistant supports that. Read-only access is enough.
- Your location, whether you need work authorization or sponsorship, how many hours a week you have for learning, and your budget

---

## The 5 steps

### 1. Collect your evidence
Gather the resume, the job descriptions and the replies in one conversation. Even 10 to 20 applications are enough to start seeing patterns.

### 2. Score your fit honestly
For each role:
- **Strong:** you meet every must-have
- **Close:** 1 or 2 learnable things are missing
- **Stretch:** the gap is years, a title or a domain

Name the **one biggest gap**, and quote the wording from the posting.

### 3. Find the pattern
- Group your roles into families, using your own field's language.
- For each family, count how many you applied to, how many replied at all, and how many led to a screen or interview.
- Note which gaps keep showing up in roles that rejected you or never replied.

### 4. Close the right gaps first (learnable gaps)
Rank the gaps you can learn by this score:

**priority = how often the gap appears × (1 + share of those roles that rejected or ignored you) ÷ weeks to close it**

For each of your top 3 gaps:
- **Quick win:** a free or low-cost course
- **Credential:** only if it's real and relevant to the postings
- **Proof project:** one small piece of work you can show, built from your real experience

### 5. Retarget what you can't learn quickly
Years of experience, licenses, supervised hours, sponsorship and seniority can't be fixed with a course. Aim one level down, or at a nearby role where you're already strong. For licensed work, look up the official licensing board in your state or country for the real route and timeline.

Repeat every week. Update your statuses and re-rank your gaps.

## Copy-paste prompt

> Here is my resume, [N] job descriptions I applied to, and the responses I received. I'm based in [location] and [do / do not] need work sponsorship. I have [X] hours a week and a budget of [$Y] for learning.
>
> 1. For each role, rate my fit as strong, close or stretch, and name the one biggest gap, quoting the posting.
> 2. Group the roles into families and show, for each family, how many I applied to, how many replied, and how many led to a screen or interview.
> 3. Label every gap as one of: learnable skill, credential, license or regulated requirement, experience (years or title), or eligibility (authorization, location, language).
> 4. Rank the learnable and credential gaps by how often they appear × (1 + the share that appear in roles that rejected me or never replied) ÷ the weeks needed to close them.
> 5. For the top 3, suggest a free course, a relevant certification, and a small proof project based on my real experience.
> 6. For license, experience and eligibility gaps, suggest roles I should target instead.
>
> Rules: only recommend courses, certifications or licenses you can confirm on the provider's or issuing body's official site, and include the link. Write "not verified" if you can't confirm one. Do not invent prices, durations or requirements. Never suggest adding skills to my resume that I don't have.

---

## Ground rules
- ✅ **Check every link yourself.** AI tools can describe courses and certifications that don't exist, or quote prices that are out of date.
- ✅ **Go to the source:** the professional association, the vendor, a university, or the licensing board.
- ✅ **Never claim a skill until you've closed the gap.**
- ✅ **Be honest about small samples.** Five applications don't make a trend.
- ✅ **Keep it private.** Remove names, phone numbers and email addresses before you paste anything into a shared tool.

---

## Weekly rhythm (about 20 minutes)
1. Add this week's applications and replies.
2. Re-run the prompt.
3. Check the action items: pending assessments, questionnaires and recruiter questions.
4. Pick one gap to work on for the next two weeks.

The goal isn't more applications. It's fewer, better-matched ones.
